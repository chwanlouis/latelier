L'atelier.

Le Guide Culinaire Expert Engine Output

Recipe Target
Ordinary Consommé
Yield Volume
Four Quarts (4L)
Execution Time
4 - 5 Hours Simmer

Ingredients

  • Shin of Beef3 lbs
  • Lean Beef3 lbs
  • Fowls' Carcases1.5 lbs
  • Carrots1 lb
  • Turnips1 lb
  • Leeks0.5 lb
  • Celery1 stick
  • Parsnips0.5 lb
  • Medium Onion (w/ 1 Clove)1 unit
  • Grey Salt0.5 oz
  • Water5 Quarts

Preparation Protocol

  1. String the beef together, place into a stock-pot with the poultry carcases, 5 quarts of water, and grey salt.
  2. Heat over a moderate fire, stirring occasionally. Do not let it boil too quickly to ensure proper albumin coagulation.
  3. Carefully remove the scum (albumen) as it forms on the surface. Add a little cold water before boiling point to retard it and help force out remaining scum.
  4. Add the vegetable garnishing, remove their scum, and wipe the edge of the stock-pot clean to the fluid level.
  5. Move to a corner of the fire and simmer slowly for 4 to 5 hours.
  6. Remove from heat, add half a pint of cold water, let rest for a few minutes, skim the surface grease completely, and strain through a fine strainer into a wide tureen. Leave uncovered in a draught to cool rapidly.
